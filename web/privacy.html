Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 29, 2026 | Last Updated: July 29, 2026

Welcome to Aivy ("we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and business information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use the Aivy application and our Meta WhatsApp Business integration.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide our intelligent CRM and WhatsApp messaging assistant services:

  • Account & Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and Google OAuth profile details when you sign in.
  • WhatsApp Business Data: WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID, Phone Number ID, display phone number, and OAuth access tokens generated via Meta Embedded Signup.
  • Messaging & Interactions: Customer messages, text queries, reminder schedules, and payment metadata processed through Aivy to assist with business operations.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to provide, operate, and improve Aivy's features:

  • To send and receive WhatsApp messages on behalf of your business via Meta Cloud API.
  • To generate automated business reminders, payment collection logs, and customer stats.
  • To authenticate users and secure access to your business records.

3. Data Security & Storage

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your credentials and data:

  • Secret Management: Sensitive Meta access tokens are stored securely in Google Cloud Secret Manager and are never exposed to client-side code.
  • Database Protection: Business records are secured using Firebase Firestore rules restricting access exclusively to authenticated owners.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is shared only with service providers required to operate the application:

  • Meta / WhatsApp Cloud API: To route and deliver messaging payload.
  • Google Cloud Platform & Firebase: For hosting, cloud computing, and database management.

5. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You have the right to access, modify, or request deletion of your account and WhatsApp connection data at any time. You can disconnect your WhatsApp Business account via the Aivy app interface or submit a data deletion request by contacting us.
